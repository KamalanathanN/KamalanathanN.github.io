About Me

I am a passionate and driven Robotics and Autonomy graduate student at Drexel University with a strong foundation in electronics and communication. My professional experience as a Robotics Software Engineer and intern has allowed me to dive deep into developing autonomous systems, from last-mile delivery robots to reality capture solutions.

I thrive on the challenges of integrating perception, planning, and control to create intelligent machines. My work involves extensive use of ROS, C++, and Python, and I am always excited to explore the boundaries of sim-to-real transfer and human-robot interaction. This portfolio is a showcase of my journey and the projects I've poured my passion into.
